
📊 Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

A Sales Dashboard that utilizes Microsoft Power BI to generate business performance analytics from sales data on multiple dimensions, including sales, profitability, quantity of products, product categories, and payment methods.

An Interactive Sales Dashboard provides users with a clear, dynamic representation of the most important metrics related to sales. Users can review sales trends (month over month), determine the highest-performing products and use data-driven methods to make decisions based on the visualizations and functionalities of the filters available in the dashboard.
